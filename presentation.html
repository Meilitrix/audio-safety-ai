Abhi AI

Real-time audio threat detection for ride-hailing safety. A dual-AI system that listens for danger in Pakistani vehicles — screams, slaps, distress keywords in Urdu, English, Punjabi and Pashto — and escalates before it's too late.

Threat Detection Dual AI Engine Multilingual Real-Time
The Problem

Passengers are unsafe — and no one is listening

  • 1 in 3 women experience harassment during ride-hailing trips in Pakistan. Most incidents are audio-based — screams, slaps, verbal abuse — but go undetected.
  • No real-time monitoring exists in current ride-hailing apps. SOS buttons require manual action. By the time a passenger can tap, the threat has escalated.
  • Language barrier: Global AI models don't understand Urdu, Punjabi, or Pashto distress keywords like "bachao", "madad", "nok".
  • Vehicle cabin acoustics are a unique environment — engine noise, road vibration, enclosed space — that generic models aren't trained for.
Our Solution

An AI that listens for danger — so passengers don't have to act

Abhi AI runs a passive audio monitor during every ride. It doesn't wait for the passenger to press a button. It detects threats automatically through sound.

🎧

Acoustic Threat Detection

YAMNet + custom SVM classifier identifies screams, slaps, glass breaking, and danger sounds in real-time.

💬

Multilingual Keyword Spotting

150+ distress keywords across English, Urdu, Punjabi, Pashto — severity-weighted (L1/L2/L3).

📈

Temporal Escalation

Danger dial accumulates risk over time — repetition, escalation, and combination bonuses trigger alerts.

Innovation

What makes this different

Temporal Accumulation Model
Unlike binary SAFE/DANGER classifiers, our scoring engine accumulates risk over time. A single scream raises the dial. Repeated screams add a repetition bonus. Rising intensity adds escalation. Multiple signals firing together add combination bonus.
Domain-Specific Training
Custom SVM trained on 40 labeled audio samples from vehicle-cabin-relevant categories. Features: MFCCs, energy, ZCR, spectral contrast, chroma. 87.7% cross-validation accuracy.
Multilingual Distress Taxonomy
150+ keywords across 4 languages, severity-weighted:
L1: Mild "help", "stop"
L2: Severe "bachao", "maray"
L3: Critical "maar daalay"
Runs Fully Offline
No paid API dependency. YAMNet runs locally via TensorFlow. Custom SVM via scikit-learn. Only Web Speech API uses cloud (browser-native). Works in low-connectivity areas.
